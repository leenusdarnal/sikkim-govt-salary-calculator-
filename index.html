₹Sikkim Salary Calculator

Project in progress

A clearer view of your government salary.

A transparent, source-backed salary calculator for Government of Sikkim employees. The project is being developed to explain salary components, deductions and pay progression with references to applicable official orders.

The calculator is not available yet.This site is the initial project landing page. Salary rates and calculation features will be added only after the relevant rules are verified against official sources.

Planned features

  • Pay-matrix and basic-pay lookup
  • Estimated gross salary, deductions and net salary
  • Transparent calculation breakdowns and effective dates
  • Increment, promotion and arrears estimates
  • Printable salary statement and source-linked rules library

Accuracy and privacy

Results will be informational estimates, not official payroll records. The planned calculator is client-side and will not require an account. Do not enter confidential employee, identity or banking information.

Official orders and department instructions take precedence over this project.

Independent community project; not an official Government of Sikkim application.
